# Design notes — SG3525 PWM high-side fan controller

All values below are extracted from the schematic in `hardware/PWMFanController.epro` (the source of truth).

## 1. Topology

High-side **P-channel** switching of a 12 V fan. PWM is generated by an **SG3525A** voltage-mode controller (U1), level-shifted by a 2N7002 (Q4) and driven by a **TC4420** 6 A driver (U2) into three paralleled **120P03** P-FETs (Q1–Q3).

P-channel high-side was chosen because it holds **100 % duty indefinitely** (needed for the FULLSPEED mode) without a bootstrap/charge pump, and the fan stays ground-referenced as in the OEM loom.

```
VCC ──┬── S [Q1..Q3, P-ch 30 V 3.1 mΩ] D ──┬── PWM pad → FAN+
      │            gates ◄─ 10 Ω each      ├─ D4 MBR60100DC (flyback → GND)
   TVS + bulk         ▲                    └─ PWM LED
      │            TC4420 ◄─ Q4 ◄─ SG3525
     GND ══════════════════════════════════ FAN− (vehicle ground)
```

## 3. Oscillator & duty

SG3525 timing: `f_osc ≈ 1 / (CT · (0.7·RT + 3·RD))` with RT = R4 = 10 kΩ, CT = C7 = 4.7 nF and the discharge pin strapped to CT (RD = 0):

$$f_{osc} \approx \frac{1}{4.7\,nF \times 0.7 \times 10\,k\Omega} \approx 30\,kHz$$

OUTA/OUTB each run at f/2 and alternate; diode-ORing them (D1/D3 into R5) recombines the stream into a single **~30 kHz** PWM signal — above audibility, low enough for negligible switching loss with a 6 A driver.

Duty: the error amp is a unity-gain follower (COMP→INV), so the pot wiper voltage (0–5.1 V) maps across the CT ramp (~0.9–3.3 V) → **0 to ~96 %** combined duty (each output maxes at 49 % of the pair-period; internal dead-time prevents true 100 %). Genuine 100 % is available only through the FULLSPEED trigger, which bypasses the controller.

## 4. Soft-start

C9 = 100 µF on pin 8, charged by the internal ~50 µA source:

$$\frac{dV}{dt} = \frac{50\,\mu A}{100\,\mu F} \approx 0.5\,V/s \quad\Rightarrow\quad t_{ramp} \approx \frac{3.3 - 0.9\,V}{0.5\,V/s} \approx 5\,s$$

Output duty starts moving after ~2 s and reaches the pot setting after ~5–7 s. The shutdown pin discharges C9, so **every** enable (power-up in OEM mode, or grounding SHUTDOWN in standalone mode) is soft. This removes fan inrush from the OEM "low speed" feed entirely.

## 5. Gate drive

- **Off (default / fail-safe):** R7 (10 k) holds GATE at VCC and R10 (1 k) holds TC4420_IN at VCC → V_GS = 0. Any single failure (U1, Q4, U2 unpowered) leaves the fan off.
- **On:** SG3525 output high → Q4 sinks TC4420_IN → TC4420 (non-inverting) pulls GATE to 0 V → V_GS ≈ −12 V, fully enhancing the P-FETs. 6 A peak drive through 10 Ω per gate cleanly switches 3× 130 nC of gate charge (~30 kHz edges well under 1 µs).
- **V_GS rating note:** the 120P03 gate sees the full rail. At 12–14 V this is within ±20 V V_GS, but a load-dump transient above ~20 V would over-stress the gates — the SMCJ16A clamping at 26 V is marginal here; see §8 validation.

## 6. Power stage & thermals

3× 3.1 mΩ in parallel ≈ **1.0 mΩ** effective. At a 25 A (≈300 W fan) load:

- Total conduction loss ≈ 25² × 0.001 ≈ **0.65 W**, ~0.22 W per TO-252 → ~20 °C rise on a modest copper pour. Heatsink-free.
- Positive R_DS(on) tempco self-balances static sharing; keep the layout symmetric for even dynamic sharing.
- D4 (dual 60 A Schottky) carries the freewheel current each off-phase; at high duty its average dissipation is small, at low duty ≈ I_fan × 0.84 V × (1−D).

## 7. Input filtering & protection stack

| Stage | Part | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TVS | SMCJ16A | Clamps normal automotive spikes; **not** rated for a full unsuppressed load dump |
| HF filter | C1 1 µF + C2 10 µF | Local decoupling at the input |
| Bulk | 4× 680 µF polymer | Supplies the chopped fan current; ~15.6 A ripple capability |
| Flyback | MBR60100DC | Output → GND, catches inductive kick every cycle |
| Gate fail-safe | R7 / R10 pull-ups | Fan off on any drive failure |
| Fuse / reverse battery | **external** | A reverse connection forward-biases D2/D4 — the upstream fuse must blow. Fuse VCC appropriately |

## 8. Trigger input protection (proposal)

> Status: **proposed, not yet in the schematic** — the sheet already carries the note *"Add zener to protect 12 V on triggers"*. Needed because the EXT_CNTL pins may land on a floating/faulted ECU output and see battery voltage.

The two inputs have different exposure:

| Input | Idle state today | 12 V fault result |
|---|---|---|
| SHUTDOWN (pin 1) | 5.1 V via R2 10 k | Exceeds SG3525 pin-10 rating → U1 damage |
| FULLSPEED (pin 2) | **VCC (12–14 V)** via R10 1 k, carries the 30 kHz PWM waveform | Pin tolerates 12 V, but the *harness* is exposed to a live switching node; an ECU clamping it below VCC partially turns the FETs on |

### SHUTDOWN — series R + Zener clamp

```
JST pin 1 ──[R_s 1 kΩ]──┬── SHUTDOWN net (R2 10 k → VREF, C8 1 µF, U1 pin 10)
                        ├── BZX84C5V1 ─→ GND   (clamp)
```

- 12 V fault: clamped at 5.1 V, R_s limits the Zener to ~7 mA (48 mW — fine in 0603/0805).
- Negative transient: Zener forward-conducts, clamping at −0.7 V.
- Function preserved: grounding through R_s leaves the pin at `5.1 V × 1k/11k ≈ 0.46 V` — below the SG3525 shutdown threshold (~0.7 V).

### FULLSPEED — buffer it into the 5.1 V domain

A Zener alone **cannot** be used here: R6 is 0 Ω, so clamping the connector would clamp TC4420_IN below VCC and hold the FETs partially on. Instead, remove R6 and decouple the trigger with a two-transistor buffer:

```
JST pin 2 ──[1 kΩ]──┬── FULL_IN (10 k pull-up → VREF, BZX84C5V1 → GND)
                    │
              gate of Q5 (2N7002), source → GND
                    drain ──┬── 100 k pull-up → VREF
                            └── gate of Q6 (2N7002), source → GND
                                       drain ──→ TC4420_IN
```

- Pin grounded → Q5 off → Q6 gate = 5.1 V → Q6 sinks TC4420_IN (12 mA through R10 — trivial for a 2N7002) → 100 % output. Behaviour identical to today.
- Pin open → FULL_IN = 5.1 V → Q5 on → Q6 off → no influence.
- The connector pin now idles at **5.1 V**, survives 12 V/negative faults, and no longer exposes the internal PWM node to the harness.

**Alternative (full isolation):** a small optocoupler per input (e.g. LTV-817: LED from a protected 5.1 V feed to the connector pin, phototransistor pulling SHUTDOWN / TC4420_IN low). Costs two parts more but makes the triggers indifferent to any ECU voltage, including shorts to battery.

## 9. Optional direct ECU PWM input (proposal)

Goal: let an ECU command fan speed directly with its own PWM (typically **100–500 Hz**, and possibly open-drain pulled to 12 V, 5 V push-pull or 12 V push-pull). The TC4420 gate driver **stays in circuit** in both options — only the point where the command enters changes.

### Common input conditioning (both options)

Normalise the unknown ECU signal into a clean 0/5.1 V logic swing:

```
ECU PWM ──[10 kΩ]──┬── BZX84C5V1 → GND (clamps 12 V / negative)
                   ├── optional 10 k pull-up → VREF  (solder-jumper: fit for open-drain ECUs)
                   └── comparator (e.g. LMV331), Vth ≈ 2.5 V from a VREF divider, small hysteresis
                          └──→ PWM_ECU (0 / 5.1 V)
```

This accepts all three source types without rework: 5 V and 12 V push-pull both cross the 2.5 V threshold; open-drain works once the pull-up jumper is fitted.

### Option B — analog injection at the error amp (recommended)

Filter `PWM_ECU` to DC and feed it to the SG3525 in place of the pot wiper:

```
PWM_ECU ──[10 k]──┬──[10 k]──┬──→ SEL jumper ──→ U1 pin 2 (was: pot wiper)
                 4.7 µF     4.7 µF
                  GND        GND        (2-pole RC, fc ≈ 3.4 Hz)
```

- ECU duty → DC voltage → SG3525 duty. The **fan still switches at ~30 kHz** (silent) regardless of the ECU's 100–500 Hz rate.
- **Soft-start and all fail-safes retained**; ripple at 100 Hz is attenuated ~800× by the two poles (a slightly slower response ≈ 100 ms is irrelevant for a fan).
- SHUTDOWN must be held active for this mode — fit EXT_CNTL_JMP, or wire the ECU's enable to JST pin 1 (protected per §8).
- A 3-pad solder jumper (`SEL`: wiper / ECU) selects the source; scale note — 100 % ECU duty gives 5.1 V, ceiling-limited to the SG3525's ~96 % max. For true 100 % the ECU can additionally assert the FULLSPEED trigger.
- Inverted-logic ECU outputs simply invert the speed map — swap comparator inputs (or a second solder jumper) if needed.

### Option A — direct gate-path injection (simpler, with caveats)

Use `PWM_ECU` exactly like the FULLSPEED trigger: a 2N7002 pulling **TC4420_IN** low while the ECU commands ON (leave the SG3525 in shutdown — jumper out, JST pin 1 open).

- Duty passes through 1:1, including genuine 0 % and 100 %.
- But the **fan is chopped at the ECU's 100–500 Hz**: audible growl/tick is likely, the bulk caps and flyback diode see much longer conduction pulses, and there is **no soft-start**.

Recommendation: **Option B**. Option A is only preferable if exact 1:1 duty tracking is a requirement and the acoustic behaviour at low frequency proves acceptable on the bench.

## 10. Open items to validate before build/install

1. **Load dump:** SMCJ16A clamps at ~26 V — above the FETs' 30 V V_DS but above their ±20 V V_GS budget at the gate pull-up. Confirm the vehicle has central suppression, or fit a lower-clamp TVS / gate Zener.
2. **Fan stall current:** verify the external fuse and D4 ratings against the fan's stall draw.
3. **SHUTDOWN threshold margin:** confirm 0.46 V grounded-through-1 k level against the actual SG3525 batch (datasheet threshold ~0.7–1 V).
4. **Thermals:** confirm FET pour temperature at sustained 100 % duty, engine bay ambient.
5. **EMC:** 30 kHz high-side chopping with no output snubber — check radiated noise on the fan harness; add an RC snubber across D4 if needed.
